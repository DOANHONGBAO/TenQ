How TenQ.ai uses data

  • No accounts. We do not ask for a name, email address, or password.
  • Anonymous cookie. A signed, HttpOnly cookie (tenq_sid) holds a random identifier so your questions and result for the day stay with your browser. It lasts up to one year. Clearing your cookies removes it.
  • Bot protection. Cloudflare Turnstile runs an invisible check once when a session starts. Cloudflare processes signals from your browser for this purpose under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • IP address. Your IP address is used only to limit abuse. We store it only as a keyed hash that changes every day, never in plain form, and it expires within a day.
  • Questions. Your questions are sent to an AI service (Ollama Cloud) to be answered, and are kept with your game for at most two days. The leaderboard shows only an anonymous name such as Player-7F3A9B.
